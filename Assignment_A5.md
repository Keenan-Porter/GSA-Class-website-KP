I created the Particles based on something that I knew quite well and could mostly do from memory. That being the plasma pistol bolt from Halo: CE Anniversary as that was one of my first halo experiences and I always thought the effect was cool. I made it a particle because in another edition, Halo: Reach, there's a scene where a bunch of plasma rounds are flying across the screen like particles. I liked the idea so I made the particle effect using some of the basic shapes and then manually adding a tail. I did it manually mostly because I didn't remember that the feedback function existed. Still I think this gave the project a cool effect for the particles. I made them move at different speeds to try and give the illusion of depth. I liked the result. I also think you could modify it to look more like a spaceship traveling past the Speed of Light.

The feedback I got was mostly to my expectations as people thought it was cool. It was pointed out to me that the feedback function was an option, which I think may or may not work and I would like to try out. No one was able to guess that it was from Halo but I think what I did was particularly niche since its not something people would immediately recognize.

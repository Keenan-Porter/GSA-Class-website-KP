When I was creating the different vants I wanted to explore some behavior that was effectively randomized. I started just by making a vant that turns left instead of right to created different variations by default. But then I made a vant that has a count that is multiplied to how much it rotates. This is only applied in certain situations, making it go straight for a period of time. 

This however, did not result in the behavior I was expecting. At first, the vants seem to do nothing, if another vant touches the area that they circle, it begins to show more complex behavior. Eventually, all of the "random" vants begin to move wildly. I think this may have to do with some of the math that the computer is doing calculating the multiplication between the default rotation and the timer that is increasing. This makes interesting results by itself but the vants change behavior two more times. The next behavior they start performer is that they begin to move up and down, leaving dashes of pheromones. This is a strange visual. The final version of their activity, is that they simply start moving downward, inverting the pheromone value of every cell they make contact with. I have no idea how th is happens, and nothing seems to change their movements. Its really interesting to watch so I think its worth keeping.
